# Juneau, Alaska
*A song celebrating the spirit of Juneau*

---

**Genre:** Pacific Northwest Folk
**Mood:** Majestic, Reflective, Proud
**Suno Tags:** Style of Music: pacific northwest folk with orchestral swells, mandolin, majestic vocals

[Intro tag]
Capital city on the water, glacier-carved and proud...

[Verse 1]
Perched between the mountains and the Gastineau Channel's gleam,
Juneau holds the capital like a monarch holds a dream.
No roads can bring you here, you've got to fly or take the ferry's call,
Mendenhall Glacier whispers to the salmon in the fall.

[Pre-Chorus]
Tlingit warriors carved their stories in the cedar and the stone,
This is land where only waterways have ever called it home.

[Chorus]
Juneau, Juneau — capital standing tall,
Where the glaciers meet the ocean and the eagles never fall.
Juneau, Juneau — proud beneath the rain,
Alaska's capital city, running through our veins.

[Verse 2]
Mount Roberts rises steeply, tramway touching sky,
Whale watching from Auke Bay as humpbacks passing by.
The Governor's mansion watches over Front Street's stride,
Tracy's Arm is calling with the silence of the tide.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Red Dog Saloon to the state legislature floor,
Juneau's been the beating heart since eighteen eighty-four.

[Chorus]
Juneau, Juneau — capital standing tall,
Where the glaciers meet the ocean and the eagles never fall.
Juneau, Juneau — proud beneath the rain,
Alaska's capital city, running through our veins.

[Bridge]
Three thousand feet of glacier, blue as ancient time,
Tlingit culture flowing through each rhythm, every rhyme.
Isolated by design but never by our will,
Juneau stands as proof that greatness stands on any hill.

[Final Chorus]
Juneau, Juneau — capital standing tall,
Where the glaciers meet the ocean and the eagles never fall.
Juneau, Juneau — stronger than the storm,
In the capital of Alaska, every heart is warm.

[Outro]
Mendenhall waters shimmer... Juneau standing proud...
Juneau... Juneau... capital of the north.
